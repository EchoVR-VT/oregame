Ore Duels

Ready to battle Glungus Bot?

  • You and the bot each start with 20 ♥ health.
  • Every ore is a unit with its own ⚔ attack and ♥ health. Cost: Common 1, Rare 2, Epic 3, Legendary 4, Mythic 5, Exotic 6 energy. Golden ores get +1/+1, Rainbow +2/+2, Hacker +3/+3 and Checkered +5/+5.
  • Drag an ore from your hand onto an empty slot (or click the card, then click a slot).
  • Click a glowing ore, then click an enemy ore to attack it. The attacker takes no damage back. If the enemy has no ores, hit their hero directly.
  • Ores can attack the turn they are played. When your deck runs out, your discard pile is reshuffled into it. You get 60 seconds per turn and the duel lasts 20 rounds (higher health wins).
  • Win a duel to earn packs: Easy 1, Medium 5, Hard 10.

Build a deck in the Deck tab, then start. (More ores in the deck = more cards to draw.)
